BC Building Code 2024 · PDF vs. website

Where the website differs from the signed PDF

We compared every provision, table cell, note, clause, figure and equation of the signed MO package PDF (MO Package BCBC MRK signed.pdf) with the same content on the BC Building Code website. This report lists the differences that come from the website: how it renders references, standards, punctuation, formatting and formulas, and places where its data or structure differs from the PDF. Each item has a count, the provisions where it concentrates, real examples, and a suggested fix. The PDF is the reference throughout.

Website: dev.buildingcode.gov.bc.ca, version 2024, content as of 2024-03-08 · First run 2026-09-27 · Updated 2026-09-28

Changes since the first run

The counts above are from the 2026-09-27 run. On 2026-09-28 the matching items rose from 82,706 to 83,548 of 94,166 (88.7%), from three kinds of change:

  • the typing and punctuation differences below are accepted as matches;
  • our PDF and web extraction was fixed where it misread the source (see What is not the site's);
  • the figure and equation comparison now sees past rendering differences, and fails the site's garbled formulas (see Figures and equations).

Structure and data

Sections 3.9 and 3.10 are swapped

2 sections and everything under them

The site files the PDF's Section 3.9 content under 3.10 and the reverse, down to article level. Its headings and titles are swapped too, so every provision in both sections sits under the wrong number.

PDFSection 3.9. Self-service Storage Buildings
Section 3.10. Objectives and Functional Statements
3.9.1.1. Definition
Site3.9 Objectives and Functional Statements
3.10 Self-service Storage Buildings
3.9.1.1 Attributions to Acceptable Solutions

Suggested fix: renumber the two sections in the site's source data.

Table C-2 is the national table, not BC's

PDF 120 rows · site 709 rows

The PDF's Table C-2 (climatic design data) lists British Columbia locations only, ending at Youbou. The site's table continues with Alberta, Ontario, Quebec, Yukon, Nunavut and the rest of Canada.

Suggested fix: confirm which table the BC code should publish. If it's the BC one, trim the site's table to BC locations.

Table 5.9.1.1. combines columns

about 190 cells

The PDF gives the issuing body, document number and title separate columns. The site repeats a combined string, the agency, number and quoted title, in each of those cells.

Figures and equations

The PDF has 588 numbered images: 485 figures and 103 equations. 448 match the site's (395 figures, 53 equations). Figures are compared by their drawing, allowing for the marked-up PDF's heavier line weight, its green revision frames and its smaller embedded copies. Equations are compared by their image and, where OCR can read the PDF's formula, by its text against the site's formula text.

The site shows garbled formulas

53 of its 133 equations

What is not the site's

A few remaining differences come from how we read the PDF or the site. They are listed here so they aren't reported as site problems:

  • About 40 cells in Tables 9.23.13.7.-B and -D. The PDF draws no line between some sub-rows, so our extraction keeps them in one row where the site has several.
  • 21 table rows split by a page break (Tables 1.1.1.1.(5), 1.3.1.2., 9.38.1.1., 9.10.3.1.-B, A-9.11.1.4.-A and -B). The rest of the row continues at the top of the next page, looking exactly like a new row under a spanning cell. 156 real new rows in 3.10.1.1., 4.5.1.1. and 9.38.1.1. look the same, so the split rows are left unjoined rather than risk those.
  • About 20 small house diagrams in 9.23.13.7. and 9.23.13.11. The PDF embeds them as tiny rasters (about 40 × 100 px) in a frame. At that size no check we tried tells the same drawing from another house variant without also passing real differences, so they stay failures.
  • About 25 equations with fractions or sums. They show the same formula on both sides, but the PDF sets them in a typewriter face and the site in MathJax, so their pictures differ, and OCR can't read stacked formulas well enough to vouch for them.
  • An unknown share of the "other wording" items may include extraction errors that weren't reviewed one by one.

Fixed on 2026-09-28

  • Text:
    • the "•" the PDF prints before list items;
    • three Articles whose headings the PDF sets in the body font (9.4.2.2., 9.10.9.19., 9.10.16.3.);
    • an "i)" after Clause (h) that starts (h)'s Subclauses (3.2.4.19.(1), 3.8.3.17.(1));
    • markers the PDF sets as their own span with no space after them (pages 45, 127, 1044);
    • Appendix D's nested Subclauses.
  • Figures and equations:
    • 137 site figures our web extraction had skipped because the site's data gives them no id of their own: Spec Table 1's wall-assembly drawings, and some Appendix D and Part 9 note figures. All 137 now match, and PDF figures without a web counterpart fell from 184 to 47;
    • the six A-9.32.3.4. figures, which the PDF draws flipped and we had saved upside down;
    • wide uncaptioned diagrams we had read as equations (A-4.1.6.16., A-9.5.3.1., A-9.8.4.);
    • 50 figures that failed only on the PDF's heavier lines, green revision frames or small copies;
    • images the two sides label differently (a formula the PDF reads as a figure in 4.1.6.5., 4.1.6.10. and 4.1.6.12.), now paired by their place in the provision;
    • 7 equations that OCR confirms read the same;
    • the viewer's highlight for a drawing a table repeats in many rows.
